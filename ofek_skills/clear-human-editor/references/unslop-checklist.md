# Unslop checklist

Use this checklist as the final prose-editing pass. Preserve meaning, evidence, uncertainty, terminology, and the author's intended level of formality. Apply equivalent patterns in the document's language rather than searching only for the English examples below.

## Rewrite process

1. Scan the draft for the patterns below.
2. Rewrite or cut each weak passage without changing its claim.
3. Restore natural rhythm and a recognizably human voice appropriate to the document.
4. Ask what still makes the result look machine-written, then fix it.

For academic and technical documents, human voice means clear judgment, concrete wording, and varied sentence rhythm. Do not add personal opinions, jokes, first person, or informality unless the author or genre calls for them.

## Content

- Cut puffery such as "pivotal moment," "testament to," "evolving landscape," and "setting the stage."
- Remove promotional adjectives unless evidence supports them.
- Replace vague attribution such as "experts believe" with a named source, or delete it.
- Remove superficial participial phrases such as "highlighting," "ensuring," or "showcasing" when they add no fact.
- Replace formulaic obstacle-and-triumph language with the actual constraint and outcome.
- Do not name-drop sources, tools, or institutions without explaining their relevance.

## Language

- Prefer plain words. Replace words such as "utilize," "leverage," and "facilitate" with "use" or "help" when that is the meaning.
- Remove stock AI vocabulary such as "crucial," "delve," "intricate," "interplay," "pivotal," "showcase," "underscore," and abstract uses of "landscape."
- Replace ornate substitutes for "is" and "has," including "serves as," "stands as," "boasts," and "features."
- Avoid the formula "not just X, but Y." State the point directly.
- Do not force ideas into groups of three.
- Use one stable term for each concept. Do not cycle through synonyms for variety.
- Remove false ranges phrased as "from X to Y" when the items do not form a real scale.
- Replace abstract technical-sounding metaphors with the concrete mechanism or object.

## Style

- Avoid em dashes. Split the sentence or use ordinary punctuation.
- Use colons for genuine introductions to lists or examples, not as a repeated sentence connector.
- Use bold text only when it improves navigation.
- Avoid list items whose bold label merely repeats the sentence that follows.
- Use sentence case for headings unless the required style guide says otherwise.
- Remove decorative emoji and chatbot flourishes.
- Use the quotation-mark style required by the document and language; do not change quotations unnecessarily.

## Plain speech

- Say what happened, how it works, or what the reader should do. Cut sentences that only describe how something feels.
- Split sentences that require rereading. Keep one main idea per sentence when possible.
- Prefer active voice when the actor matters. Passive voice is fine when the actor is unknown or irrelevant.
- Cut adverbs that prop up weak verbs. Use a measured value or a more exact verb when the evidence permits it.
- Replace filler such as "in order to," "due to the fact that," and "it is important to note that" with shorter wording or delete it.
- Reduce stacked hedges while preserving real uncertainty.
- Replace generic openings and conclusions with the document's actual subject, finding, or next step.

## Human rhythm

- Vary sentence length naturally. Do not make every paragraph follow the same cadence.
- Use concrete subjects and specific details already supported by the source.
- Allow restrained authorial judgment when the genre supports it.
- Do not make prose artificially messy. Clarity matters more than performing humanness.

## Final audit

Confirm that:

- no stock AI phrase remains without a real purpose;
- no sentence could be pasted unchanged into an unrelated document;
- no unsupported detail was added to make the prose more vivid;
- no necessary technical qualification was removed;
- the edited version still sounds appropriate for its audience and genre.
